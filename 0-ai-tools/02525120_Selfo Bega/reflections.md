Reflection



Working on the topic of AI Coding and Development Tools helped me understand how artificial intelligence is transforming the field of software engineering. During this research, I learned that AI is no longer just a future concept, but a technology that is already being used daily by programmers, companies, and students around the world. Tools such as GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT demonstrate how AI can assist developers by generating code, solving problems, and improving productivity.



One of the most interesting things I discovered is how AI can simplify difficult programming tasks. Many coding activities that normally require a lot of time, such as debugging, testing, and documentation, can now be completed much faster with the help of AI tools. This made me realize that technology is continuously evolving to make human work easier and more efficient.



At the same time, this topic also showed me that AI has limitations and challenges. AI-generated code is not always correct, and developers must still review and test the results carefully. I understood that human creativity, logical thinking, and problem-solving skills remain extremely important in software development. AI can support programmers, but it cannot completely replace them.



Researching this topic also increased my interest in programming and artificial intelligence. I learned about several modern development tools and how companies are using them in real-world applications. It made me think about the future of technology and how AI will continue to influence education, businesses, and everyday life.



Overall, this research helped me gain a better understanding of the advantages and disadvantages of AI coding tools. I believe these technologies will become even more advanced in the future and will play an important role in software engineering. However, developers will still need strong technical knowledge and critical thinking skills in order to use AI effectively and responsibly.
