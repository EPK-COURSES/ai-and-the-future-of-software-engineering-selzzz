With the development of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in the field of education to help process daily activities including teaching and learning.The objective f this study is to investigate Artificial Intelligence (AI) in education,especially in the teaching and learning process.This research uses library research. AIthat emerged as the industrial revolution is also the result of the creative minds of human natural intelligence.
